Cass County Public Safety Task Force releases final report, recommendations

Gabriel Ostler, KVRR News 3 hrs ago 0
Listen to audio version of this article
0:00 / 0:00 Instaread
Sponsored by

FARGO, N.D. (KVRR) - The Cass County Public Safety Task Force has now released their full final report and recommendations.

The task force was created back near the end of 2025 to address concerns raised by Cass County community members that year.

It consisted of a wide variety of law enforcement figures and other legal officials, from the Cass County Sheriff to the U.S. Marshal for North Dakota, and met seven times in 2026.

Its main finding: behavioral health, more than crime, is the county’s top safety concern. Members proposed five steps in response.

Chair Joel Vettel cited state and local partnerships and framed the effort around “quality of life.”

One step revives Crisis Intervention Team training for local agencies; Vettel said planning for that is underway.

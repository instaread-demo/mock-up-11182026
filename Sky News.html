American soldier suspected of spying for Russia handed to US authorities

The head of the FBI says the man is now in US custody and warns there's "zero tolerance for anyone who betrays their oath".

A US soldier arrested by UK counter terror police has been taken into custody by the American military.

The 41-year-old man was detained last week on suspicion of spying for Russia.

Sky News understands he was stationed at Imjin Barracks near Innsworth, on the outskirts of Gloucester, where NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps has its headquarters.

He had been held under the National Security Act over suspected offences involving protected information and foreign intelligence.

The Metropolitan Police searched two sites in Gloucestershire and said the case is unrelated to other counter terror inquiries.

Assistant Commissioner Laurence Taylor said the attorney general's office and the Crown Prosecution Service were consulted, calling the allegations "extremely serious".

FBI director Kash Patel credited a joint espionage investigation, warning of "zero tolerance" for anyone betraying their oath.

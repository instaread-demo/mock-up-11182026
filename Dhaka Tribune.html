Nearly 300 arrested as workers' unrest spreads in Savar-Ashulia

24 cases have been filed with Savar and Ashulia police stations in connection with the labour unrest

Ariful Islam Sabbir
Publish : 18 Nov 2026, 01:58 PMUpdate : 18 Nov 2026, 01:58 PM
Add to Preferred Sources

Workers' unrest continued in several industrial areas of Savar and Ashulia on Wednesday, with workers demanding, among other things, a minimum monthly wage of Tk30,000.

Workers attempted to block roads at two locations, prompting police to intervene. In one incident, police persuaded the workers to leave, while in another, they used tear gas shells to clear the road.

Meanwhile, 24 cases have been filed with Savar and Ashulia police stations in connection with the labour unrest, and around 300 people have been arrested on allegations of involvement in sabotage, police said.

Around 9am, Maxcom International BD Ltd workers gathered on the Hemayetpur-Singair road as Aleya Apparels Ltd and, after 8:30am, other factories halted work; they left after a police warning.

At about 10:15am, police used tear gas to clear protesters blocking the Abdullahpur-Baipail road in Narsinghapur, Ashulia Police Station officer-in-charge Mohammad Tarikul Islam said.

Speaking around 10am, Additional Superintendent Md Jahangir Alam said some factories had shut under Section 13(1) of the Bangladesh Labour Act, with 24 cases filed and around 300 people arrested on allegations of sabotage by Tuesday.

Industrial Police-1 superintendent Mohammad Mominul Islam Bhuiyan said 10 factories stayed closed under Section 13(1), seven declared a general holiday, 13 shut for lack of work and 11 suspended operations.

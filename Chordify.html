Hey there, fellow music enthusiast! Ever wondered why Chordify is nudging you to play your favorite tunes regularly? Well, it’s not just because we want to see you jamming, though that’s always a bonus! The truth is, we care about your well-being, and we believe that making music is the ultimate recipe for a healthy mind and soul.

In this article you will find:

  • Your brain loves music
  • Music is the language everyone understands
  • Nurture body and soul
  • Weekly Play Challenge

Your brain loves music

Playing exercises the mind, sharpening focus and thinking, while the brain regions involved form links that aid memory and problem solving.

Music is the language everyone understands

As a shared language across borders, music also trains listening and pronunciation.

Jamming with friends or an online community builds social bonds, so a weekly session strengthens connections too.

Nurture body and soul

The body gains too: rhythm and movement help coordination and motor control, and playing eases stress and anxiety, making each Chordify prompt a step toward calm.

Weekly Play Challenge

The invitation is to play once a week, less for the streak than for a healthier mind, with brain, language, social and stress-relief benefits along the way.

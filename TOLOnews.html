The International Criminal Court (ICC) has halted its investigations into alleged war crimes committed by US forces and ISIS-K in Afghanistan.

Reuters reported that the judges based their decision on the prosecution's failure to demonstrate that it could effectively pursue investigations into these cases.

The court's investigations will now focus on actions taken by the Islamic Emirate since 2021.

However, the ICC Prosecutor's Office has challenged the decision and requested a review.

Released court papers say the prosecutor had not shown it could pursue the inquiry further, so the chamber directed its Registry to close the case.

The Islamic Emirate has not yet responded. It has previously refused to recognize the court and called its rulings politically motivated, and on February 20, 2025, deputy spokesperson Hamdullah Fitrat said it was not bound by the Rome Statute.

The move follows earlier US sanctions on the ICC over its Afghanistan inquiry and arrest warrants for Israeli officials.

Here’s how to bring it into your home.

In 2027, the colors we choose for our homes will continue to evolve. Neutrals are getting warmer and softer, earth tones are becoming even more nature-inspired, and trending shades are both rich and bold. With all of these elements in mind, IKEA has chosen its Color of the Year 2027, and it’s about to become the focal point of your space.

The IKEA Color of the Year 2027 Is Full of Character

IKEA’s pick is Wild Fig, a deep berry hue with a botanical, moody and slightly unexpected feel.

It warms neutral furniture, wood and textiles, holds up beside yellow, blue and 2026’s Rebel Pink in maximalist rooms, and suits nature-inspired palettes.

Incorporating Wild Fig Into Your Home

IKEA’s 2027 Style Guide applies the shade to cookware, storage bins, bedding and furniture, from the PS Folding Side Table to the KRAMPHULT armrest tray.

Use it broadly on a bedspread or in accents like throw pillows; it looks softer on fabric and glossier on metal.

It also pairs with florals, natural wood, stone and greenery, such as trellis wallpaper, oak shelves or mini floral print cushions.

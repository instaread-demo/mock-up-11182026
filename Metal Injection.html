Avatar Announce 2027 Headlining Tour With Barbarians of California, Gates to Hell

"Some animals hibernate. Some of us get ready."

By Cervanté Pope
Published 13 hours ago

Not even a week after revealing their new Zakk Wylde-featuring Christmas song "Naughty List," Avatar have now shared a grip of dates for their upcoming North American headlining jaunt "Across the Underworld Tour." Coming along with them, on select dates, will be Gates to Hell and The Barbarians of California.

Frontman Johannes Eckerström says the band is primed for the run, which fits around their dates supporting Metallica, and promises a stronger Avatar come May.

Tickets are available from 10 a.m. local time on Friday, November 20, via the band's website here.

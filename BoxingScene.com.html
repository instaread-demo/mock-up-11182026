Gervonta Davis has claimed that he will face former unified lightweight champion George Kambosos in the Spring of 2027.

Davis, 30-0-1 (28 KOs), has not fought since his controversial draw with Lamont Roach in March of last year and has since been kept out of the ring due to legal problems.

Davis, 32, was due to face YouTuber-turned-boxer Jake Paul in November 2025 but the bout was axed due to a number of accusations being brought against Davis from an ex-girlfriend.

The civil case is settled, but criminal charges remain, with a November 17 court date.

His Instagram poster for the Spring bout carries the PBC logo, though the PBC has not confirmed it.

Kambosos, 22-4 (10 KOs), lost to Richardson Hitchins in June and is 2-4 since upsetting Teofimo Lopez in 2021.

He also posted an AI face-off clip captioned "real war is coming".
